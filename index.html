Talk to Refuge

Refuge listens, understands your situation, and points you toward the right Scottish organisations. A knowledgeable first step — not a replacement for Shelter Scotland or a housing solicitor.

What Refuge does: signpost to services, explain general rights, help you prepare for council conversations.

What Refuge won't do: confirm live shelter availability or give definitive legal advice. Always call to verify.

Verified services that can help

All services listed are free. Phone numbers were accurate at time of writing — verify directly before travelling.

⚠️ Always call ahead. Shelters, food banks, and clothing services change hours and availability. refugeSeek cannot confirm real-time availability. Call directly before travelling, especially in the evening.

Scotland priority
Shelter Scotland
Emergency housing advice, council application support, and guidance on rights under Scottish housing legislation. Free helpline staffed by trained advisors.
📞 0808 800 4444
Free · 24/7 · Verify at scotland.shelter.org.uk

Outreach
Simon Community Scotland
Outreach teams and night shelters across Scotland, so call first to check availability.
📞 0800 027 7466
Free · Call to confirm availability in your area

Trussell Trust Food Banks
Emergency food parcels across Scotland. A referral is usually needed from a GP, social worker, or Citizens Advice. Some locations accept self-referrals.

Salvation Army Scotland
Warm clothing, sleeping bags, hygiene packs, and emergency essentials at local centres. Also offers food and shelter referrals. Call ahead to confirm availability.

Free advice
Citizens Advice Scotland
Free, confidential guidance on housing rights, benefits, debt, and eviction under Scots law. Walk into any CAB office or call their national line.
📞 0800 028 1456
Free · Check cas.org.uk for local hours

Healthcare
NHS Scotland
NHS Scotland guidance indicates GP practices should not refuse registration solely on the basis of no fixed address. Major Scottish cities have dedicated homeless health teams.
📞 NHS 24: 111
Free · 24/7 · nhs24.scot

Know your rights

Your housing rights: the basics

Not legal advice. Call Shelter Scotland on 0808 800 4444 to check your situation.
Councils must assess your situation
Your council must assess your case. It may offer temporary accommodation while it does.
Tell the council if you have children
Say so when you apply.
You may have the right to appeal a decision
If you disagree with a decision, you can ask for a review. Time limits apply.
NHS care should generally be accessible without a fixed address
A GP should not refuse you because you have no fixed address. Call NHS 24 on 111 if you have problems.
Benefits when you have no fixed address
Citizens Advice can explain what you can claim.
Local connection can affect where you apply
Your council may send you to another area where you have a local connection.
Scotland's legal framework — overview
Since 2012, the priority need test no longer applies to homeless households.
Your circumstances still decide what help you get.
Housing (Scotland) Act 1987 (Part II) · Homelessness etc. (Scotland) Act 2003 · Homelessness (Abolition of Priority Need Test) (Scotland) Order 2012
General information, not legal advice. Shelter Scotland: 0808 800 4444.
