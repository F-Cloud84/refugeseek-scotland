refugeSeek Scotland: Privacy Statement

What this is. refugeSeek Scotland is a non-commercial prototype that helps people find shelter, food, clothing, advice and council services in Scotland. It is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call 999.

Who runs it. Olujimmy Elijah Ogunbajo, funshodee@gmail.com. This is a non-commercial project run by one person.

What we do not do. - We do not ask you to create an account. - The page does not set cookies and does not use browser local storage. - We do not store your location, your postcode or your chat messages on our own systems. Hosting logs are covered below.

Your location. The page asks for your location only if you press "Use my location". Your browser then asks for permission. If you allow it, your location is used in your browser to show nearby services. It is not sent to us. You can enter a town or postcode instead.

Postcodes and towns. When you type a town or postcode, the page recognises Scottish postcode areas and place names in your browser. It is not sent to us.

The AI assistant. Messages you type into the assistant are sent to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model, so that it can write a reply. Please do not include your full name, address, date of birth or other identifying details. Anthropic's handling of that data is set out in its own privacy terms: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Your messages are sent to a server function on this site, which passes them to Anthropic and does not store or log them. Anthropic does not receive your location.

The map. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which receives your internet address (IP address) when the map loads.

Directions. Choosing "Maps", "Waze" or "Apple" opens that service in a new page. It sends the destination you selected, and any data that service collects, under its own terms.

Hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's servers may record your internet address and the pages you request for a limited period, as part of normal hosting. The site is hosted on Vercel and its server functions run in the United States (Vercel region iad1, Washington, D.C.). Server-side logs from the site's functions are kept for about one hour.

Information accuracy. Service details are checked by hand. Each listing shows when it was last checked 23 November 2026. Phone numbers and opening times change, so please check before you travel, and call ahead where you can.

Legal information. The housing-rights content is general guidance and is not legal advice. It has not yet been reviewed by a qualified Scottish housing solicitor. For advice on your situation, contact Shelter Scotland on 0808 800 4444 (free, 24/7).

Changes. We will update this statement if what the site does changes. Last updated: 23 November 2026.
